Research

Accretion in eccentric binaries

In a binary on an eccentric orbit, the separation between the stars changes continuously, so the way one star feeds the other changes too. A donor can lose mass as a wind that is denser near periastron, or fill its Roche lobe and overflow (RLOF) only part of the orbit. My M.Sc. project compares wind-fed accretion with RLOF to ask a concrete question: when does a transient accretion disk form around the accretor? I model this in Python.
