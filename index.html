01.About Me

Astrophysics researcher · M.Sc. Physics, Ariel University, Israel · M.Sc. Physics, NIT Hamirpur, India

Namaste! I'm Rohit, a physicist from a small village in Bihar, India, now exploring the cosmos at Ariel University, Israel. My journey from wondering about the stars on my village rooftop to actively researching accretion physics, black holes and galaxies is driven by curiosity.

Research Philosophy

I believe that understanding the universe is a sacred act, for nothing is as pure as knowledge. My research focuses on accretion physics in eccentric binaries,accretion physics, Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN) and galaxy evolution, combining multi-wavelength observations with computational modelling.
